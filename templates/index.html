Time Systems & Astronomical Frameworks

Theoretical Foundations

1. Universal & Rotational Time (UTC / UT1)

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is maintained by atomic clocks. UT1 reflects Earth's actual physical rotation relative to distant celestial bodies. The difference, $\Delta T = \text{TT} - \text{UT1}$, accounts for Earth's rotational decelerations.

2. Sidereal Time Frame (GMST / LST)

Sidereal time measures Earth's rotation relative to the Vernal Equinox rather than the Sun. Local Sidereal Time (LST) equals Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time adjusted for the observer’s specific geographic longitude ($\lambda$).

3. Apparent vs. Mean Solar Time

Apparent Solar Time is the direct reading of a sundial based on the Sun's hour angle. Mean Solar Time averages out variations caused by Earth's elliptical orbit and axial tilt through the Equation of Time (EoT).
