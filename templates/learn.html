Astronomical & Geomatics Visual Learning Center

Interactive visualizations explaining solar calculations, time drifts, and coordinate offsets for surveying operations.

1. Equation of Time (EoT) Curve

Variance in minutes between Apparent Solar Time (sundial) and Mean Solar Time (clock) across 365 days.

2. Solar vs. Sidereal Time Drift

Demonstrates cumulative time gain of Sidereal time (~3.94 min/day) relative to standard solar time over 30 days.

3. Solar Altitude & Azimuth Angles

Solar trajectory throughout daylight hours used for field survey astronomical observation planning.

4. Malaysian Location Longitude Offset (UTC+8)

Local solar time deviation from standard clock time across Malaysian locations relative to 120°E meridian.

5. Calendar Year vs Ordinal Day Mapping

Linear mapping of calendar months to ordinal day count used in Julian Date determinations.
