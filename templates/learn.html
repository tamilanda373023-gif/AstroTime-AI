Understanding Sidereal Time

Sidereal time is a timekeeping system that astronomers and geomatics engineers use to locate celestial objects. Unlike solar time, which is based on Earth's rotation relative to the Sun, sidereal time is measured relative to the distant stars.

The Equation of Time

The Equation of Time describes the discrepancy between two kinds of solar time: Apparent Solar Time (sundial time) and Mean Solar Time (steady clock time), caused by Earth's elliptical orbit and axial tilt.
