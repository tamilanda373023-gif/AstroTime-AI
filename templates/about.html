About AstroTime AI

AstroTime AI bridges rigorous celestial mechanics with modern artificial intelligence. Designed as an advanced geodetic platform, it empowers surveying professionals, students, and researchers with instantaneous, high-precision astronomical time conversions and intelligent field data analysis.

🪐

Geodetic & Time Engine

Executes standard astronomical algorithms to compute critical parameters dynamically without relying on static external databases:

  • ✨ Julian Dates (JD): Mathematically exact continuous day counting.
  • 🌍 GMST & LMST: Sidereal rotations scaled precisely by longitude ($15^\circ$ per hour).
  • ⏱️ Solar Time & Equation of Time: Fourier-approximated orbital corrections.
🤖

Nova AI & Spatial Mapping

Integrates cutting-edge artificial intelligence with interactive spatial mapping to streamline field operations:

  • 🗺️ Leaflet & OSM: Sub-arcsecond coordinate reliability for mapping.
  • 📄 Universal Parser: Instantly reads PDFs, Word docs, and Excel files.
  • 💬 Persistent Chats: Fully tracked conversation history stored securely.
