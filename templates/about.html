About AstroTime AI

A specialized Geomatics and Astronomical Time transformation platform designed for geodetic surveying, spatial positioning, and celestial calculations.

Julian Date Conversion

Computes continuous Julian Date (JD) values directly from UTC time, handling astronomical epochs and Gregorian calendar shifts seamlessly.

Equation of Time (EoT)

Calculates the precise difference between Apparent Solar Time (sundial) and Mean Solar Time (clock) based on orbital eccentricity and axial tilt.

Local Sidereal Time (LST)

Transforms Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time (GMST) to local longitudes to determine the hour angle of the Vernal Equinox for field observations.

AI Computational Co-Pilot

Integrated assistant offering real-time guidance on astronomical algorithms, coordinate reference frames, and dynamic process diagrams.

Technical Architecture

Engineered with a Python Flask backend for mathematical processing, SQLite via SQLAlchemy for calculation persistence, and Tailwind CSS for responsive spatial data visualization.
